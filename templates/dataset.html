Data

Breast histopathology images

Two public datasets of H&E-stained breast tissue are used. The bundled model is trained on the IDC patch dataset; the Keras pipeline targets BreakHis.

Bundled model

IDC Breast Histopathology (Janowczyk & Madabhushi, 2016)

277,524 patches of 50×50 px cut from 162 whole-slide images of breast cancer specimens scanned at 40×. Each patch is labelled by pathologist annotation as IDC-positive (invasive ductal carcinoma, the most common breast cancer subtype, ~80% of cases) or IDC-negative.

Image size
50×50 px RGB (cropped to 48×48)
Classes
0 = benign / non-IDC, 1 = malignant / IDC

BreakHis (Spanhol et al., 2016)

7,909 microscopic biopsy images (700×460 px) from 82 patients at four magnifications (40×, 100×, 200×, 400×).

Benign (2,480)
adenosis, fibroadenoma, phyllodes tumour, tubular adenoma
Malignant (5,429)
ductal, lobular, mucinous, papillary carcinoma
Preprocessing
224×224, ÷255, augmentation

Split

Patient-level train / validation / test split

Splitting by patient instead of by patch matters. Neighbouring patches from one slide look almost identical, so a random patch split would leak test patients into training and inflate the score.
